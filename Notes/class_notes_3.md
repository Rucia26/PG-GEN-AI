## Definitions
- **Outlier** – A data point that deviates significantly from other observations; can skew statistical analyses and model performance.
- **Missing Value** – An absent entry in a dataset (e.g., `NaN`, `null`, blank) that must be addressed before modeling (imputation, removal, or flagging).
- **Generative AI** – A branch of artificial intelligence focused on creating new content (text, images, code, etc.) by learning patterns from training data.
